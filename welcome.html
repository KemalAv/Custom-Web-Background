How to Use — Quick Start Guide

Follow these 5 simple steps to transform your browsing experience into a clean and personalized aesthetic.

1

Choose Background Media

Upload your favorite image (JPG, PNG, GIF) or looping video (MP4) from your computer. You can also paste any web image URL or use our built-in wallpaper.

2

Choose Transparency Mode

Select how website containers blend: None Transparent (standard solid text boxes), Glass UI (frosted blurred glass), or Transparent (full transparency).

3

Adjust Blur & Dimming

Use the Blur slider to soften wallpaper details and the Dim slider to balance brightness, or pick instant presets (Eye-Care / Deep Focus) for optimal readability.

4

Target Websites & Themes

Choose whether wallpapers appear globally across all websites or only on up to 10 specific websites (with convenient 1-click active tab addition).

5

Apply & Save

Click Apply & Save. Open any website tab (such as YouTube or Google) and enjoy your new wallpaper in real-time!

Tips & Tricks for the Best Experience

  • Auto Theme Adaptation: When Dim Overlay Color is set to Auto, the overlay color automatically follows your custom Dark or Light theme color based on your browser/system mode.

  • MP4 Video Backgrounds: The extension supports lightweight .mp4 video files for animated, living backgrounds with zero lag.
  • Quick Access: Access settings anytime by clicking the extension icon in your Chrome toolbar.
