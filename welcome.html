GET STARTED IN 3 STEPS

How to use the extension

All settings are available by clicking the extension icon in the browser toolbar.

  1. STEP 01
    ↑

    Choose a background

    Click Choose File to use a JPG, PNG, GIF, or MP4 from your device.

    OR

    Paste a media link into the URL field, then click Apply URL.

  2. STEP 02
    ☼

    Customize its appearance

    Choose Chroma Key for simple transparency or Glass UI for a glass effect.

    Blur Dim
  3. STEP 03
    ✓

    Apply & save

    Click Apply & Save. Refresh the website tab if the changes are not immediately visible.

    Apply & Save
